Professional Summary

Full Stack Robotics Engineer who takes autonomous mobile robots from raw sensor data to a reliable state estimate on real hardware, in the field. Owns the perception and localization stack of an autonomous ground vehicle in C++17 and Python (ROS 2): sensor bringup and intrinsic/extrinsic calibration, multi-camera visual odometry and SLAM, depth-image and point-cloud processing, free-space and obstacle estimation, and multi-sensor fusion through factor-graph optimization (GTSAM) on SE(3), optimized for NVIDIA Jetson edge hardware. Fluent in coordinate frames, rigid-body transforms (SO(3)/SE(3)) and 3D geometry, and comfortable bisecting a stack across sensor drivers, timing, networking, and geometric logic to find where reality and the math disagree. Builds the validation infrastructure that proves it works: Isaac Sim digital twins for simulation-first testing before every hardware deployment, large-scale ROS-bag field-data collection and replay for repeatable metrics, and 3D visualization in Foxglove and RViz to diagnose real-world failure modes. Hardware-native mechatronics background, first-author SLAM publication, and a decade in competitive robotics.
